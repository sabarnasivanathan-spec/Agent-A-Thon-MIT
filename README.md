# StudySync AI

## Project Overview

StudySync AI is an **AI-powered academic planning and recovery assistant** designed to help students manage their studies more effectively.

The system takes into account a student's **upcoming exams, subjects, topics, difficult areas, available study time, and preferences** to generate a personalized and realistic study plan.

Unlike a basic timetable generator, StudySync AI follows an **interactive, agentic approach**. Students can review the generated plan, approve it, or provide feedback for modifications. Once approved, the system helps track progress and can adapt the study plan when the student falls behind or their circumstances change.

## Core Workflow

**Student Input → Understand Requirements → Generate Study Plan → Student Review → Revise if Needed → Active Plan → Progress Check → Adapt**

## Goal

The goal of StudySync AI is to reduce the stress of figuring out **what to study, when to study, and how to recover when a student falls behind**, by providing a personalized and continuously adaptable academic plan.
